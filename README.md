# RC-PLANE

THIS my friends, is the rc plane!! it took me maybe 9 hours to create?

at first, i didnt actually know how to make an rc plane... so i looked up  a tutorial! and thats how i ended up with this design.
i built the plane, wired it up, and took it to the testing field!


**it didnt go well**


i wingtip stalled, and unfortunatly the plane plummeted into the floor. 
however, all hope was not lost! the motor (after crashing straight into the ground) actually still worked!
so i got back at it, and remade the plane. and this time bought a GYRO and a NEW RECIEVER because the reciever was the only thing that didnt survive the crash.
i glued the last piece, sent it to the flying field, and OH MY DAYS IT WAS FLYING!! THATS RIGHT, IT FLEW!! it soared through the sky, like nothing else ive ever built. however. there were 3 problems. the first problem, was obvious about a minute after flying.

**problem one**
whenever i went above 30 percent throttle, the gyro would grossly overcorrect and at one point i was sure the wings were about to rip off! at this point, i didnt know why this was...

**problem two**
after the overcorrection was getting worse, i issued a mayday and prepared for an emergency landing. if i didnt make this, we were gonna lose the plane! after an emergency landing i discovered that the reason the gyro was overcorrecting so much, was that THE MOTOR MOUNT HAD COLLAPSED!!
instead of the propellor facing forward, IT WAS FACING 30 DEGREES DOWN!!!

**problem 3** when i went to take the battery out, i touched it, and was met with somthing from the depths of boiling water tempurature! it was so hot!! im sure i got a first degree burn. but as it turns out, it wasnt the battery that was overheating... IT WAS THE ESC!!! by smart ahh forgot to add ventilation for the esc, and so it overheated TO SUCH TEMPURATURE that **it melted the hot glue**

i went back home, and added an intake, remade the motor mount, and recalibrated the gyro. and voila!! it flew gracefully and amazingly, cutting through the air like a ballarina.
